Smart Indexing
Dual-Indexing Strategy
Index 1

Transcript Chunks

~1,000 character segments, split at sentence boundaries. For granular detail retrieval.

"What was my exact LDL in March 2024?"
Index 2

Full Summaries

Structured medical summaries with diagnoses, medications, metrics. For high-level concept matching.

"Am I getting healthier?"

All queries filtered by patient_id in ChromaDB metadata — complete data isolation. Multi-tenant ready.
